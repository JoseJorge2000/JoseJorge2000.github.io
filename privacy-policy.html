Privacy Policy — Castle Survivor

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Castle Survivor is developed by JJ7Gameplays. This policy explains what the game collects and why. It is short because the game collects very little.

What we collect

We do not ask you for, or store, any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no contact form.

Your progress — coins, unlocked levels, best wave and settings — is saved on your own device. On web versions this uses your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device except as described below, and uninstalling the app or clearing your browser deletes it.

Advertising

The Android version may show optional rewarded video advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. You are never required to watch one: they are offered in exchange for an in-game bonus and can always be declined.

To serve these advertisements, AdMob may access your device's advertising ID and approximate, non-precise location (country or region level), and may use this to show more relevant advertising. We do not receive, store or have access to this data ourselves.

  • Google's advertising policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings › Privacy › Ads on your device.

Purchasing the ad-free upgrade permanently disables all advertising in the game.

Browser versions hosted on partner sites such as CrazyGames may show advertising supplied by that site, under that site's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Payments for in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is rated for teens and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
