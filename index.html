Castle Survivor

Roguelite kingdom defense — by JJ7Gameplays

Gather wood, stone and iron by day. Build and upgrade towers. Survive the night. Pick an upgrade and do it again — through escalating levels or an endless survival mode.

  • Three tower types, each with its own ability and a Wood → Stone → Iron ladder
  • Four enemy archetypes that pick their targets differently, plus bosses
  • Roguelite upgrade cards after every wave
  • Sessions of about three minutes

In development. Android release coming soon.
